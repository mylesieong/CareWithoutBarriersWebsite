Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Applies to the Care Without Barriers app on the App Store and Google Play (app ID com.mediparrot.app), version 0.2 and later.

The short version

  • Your conversations, summaries and health card are stored only on your phone. There is no account.
  • To transcribe, translate, summarize or read aloud, audio or text is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to OpenAI and keeps no copy.
  • Voice recordings are deleted from your phone as soon as each message is ready.
  • We collect five anonymous usage counts. You can turn this off in the app.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.

1. Who we are

The Care Without Barriers app (“the app”) is provided by Care Without Barriers (“we”, “us”), a community organization based in Metro Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Our Privacy Officer, Lynette Lee, is responsible for how we handle personal information. Send questions, requests and complaints to contact@carewithoutbarriers.ca, marked “Attention: Privacy Officer”.

The app helps patients who are more comfortable in Cantonese, Mandarin, Korean, Japanese, Tagalog or Punjabi talk with a doctor who speaks English or French.

2. What stays on your phone

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server of ours.

  • Conversations — the text of what you and your doctor said, both the original and the translation, and any corrections you made.
  • Summaries — take-home summaries, follow-ups and highlights generated after a visit.
  • Health card — the medicines, allergies, conditions and other notes you choose to write.
  • Visit notes — the reason for a visit and questions you prepared.
  • Consent record — which version of the notice you and your doctor agreed to, and when.
  • Settings — your languages, script, text size, auto-delete choice and analytics choice.
  • Read-aloud audio — a cache of spoken translations, so a message plays again without being sent a second time.

Recordings. When you hold a button and speak, the app records a short audio clip. The clip is deleted from your phone as soon as its message has been transcribed and translated. If sending fails, the clip is kept only so you can retry; it is deleted once the retry succeeds or you delete the message.

Translate-only visits. If you choose a translate-only visit, the conversation is held in the phone's memory while the app is open and is never written to storage.

Your phone's operating system may include the app's data in a device backup you have turned on (for example iCloud or Google backup). Those backups are controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

3. What leaves your phone, and why

Some features need a speech or language model that is too large to run on a phone. For those features only, the app sends the minimum needed to our server, which forwards it to our provider and returns the result. Our server does not store the content of any request.

When you…What is sentPurpose
Hold a button and speakThe audio clip, the two languages, and words from your health card (to help recognise medicine names)Transcribe and translate the message
Correct a message, or open your health card in a visitThe text to translate and the two languagesTranslate it
Ask for a summaryThe text of the conversation and, if you have one, your health cardWrite the summary
Tap playThe text to read and its languageProduce spoken audio

Each request also carries a fixed app key that lets our server recognise the app. It does not identify you or your phone.

Server logs. For reliability, our server records the type of request, its status, how long it took and the language pair. It never records audio, text, health-card content or summaries.

4. Service providers

We use the following providers to run the features above. They process data on our behalf under their business terms.

  • OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) — speech recognition, translation, summaries and read-aloud. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models. Under its standard API terms, OpenAI may keep requests and responses for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes them.
  • Vercel Inc. (United States) — hosts our server. Vercel keeps the content-free request logs described above for a limited period under its standard log retention.
  • Google LLC (United States) — Google Analytics receives the anonymous usage counts described below.

5. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand whether the app is useful, it counts five things: the app being opened, a visit being started, a past visit being reopened, a message being translated, and a message being read aloud. Each count is sent with a random ID created on your phone and a session number.

  • The counts never include what was said, your name, your languages or anything from your health card.
  • They pass through our server, so Google receives our server's address, not your phone's.
  • Statistics are on by default. Turn them off at any time under Settings → Your data → Share anonymous usage statistics. Deleting everything also resets the random ID.

6. What we never collect

No account, name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or health-insurance number. We do not sell or rent personal information, show advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

7. Phone permissions

  • Microphone — used only while you hold a speak button. You can revoke it in your phone's settings; the app will ask again when you next try to speak.
  • Internet — to send the requests described in section 3.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone, data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single visit, choose auto-delete for older visits, or use Delete everything, which removes all conversations, summaries, your health card, cached audio and your consent record. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone.
  • On our server, request content is held only in memory for as long as it takes to answer, then discarded.
  • At providers, retention is set as described in section 4.

Because we do not keep your conversations, we cannot recover them for you once they are deleted from your phone.

9. Security

All traffic between the app, our server and our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data on your phone is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and your device's screen lock; we recommend you use a passcode. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit risk mainly by not holding your information at all.

10. Where data is processed

Our server and our providers are located in the United States. Audio and text sent for processing may therefore be handled under U.S. law, including laws that could allow access by authorities there. By using the features in section 3 you understand this transfer. We choose providers that protect data under contract and encrypt it in transit.

11. Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law, including British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, and complain about how we handle it. In practice, everything about you is on your own phone, where you can see, correct and delete it yourself. For any request or question, contact us at the address in section 1. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian may use it on a child's behalf during the child's appointment.

13. Not medical advice

Care Without Barriers is a communication aid, not a certified medical interpreter and not a medical device. Translations and summaries can contain mistakes. Always confirm dosages, allergies and procedures with your care provider, and ask for a professional interpreter for serious or complex decisions.

14. Changes to this policy and contact

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant — for example, a new kind of data leaving your phone — the app will ask for your consent again before it applies.
