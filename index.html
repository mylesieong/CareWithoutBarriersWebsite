Two buttons. One conversation.

You hold the phone between you and your doctor. Each of you has your own button.

  1. Hold and speak

    Press your button and talk in your language. Let go when you're done.

  2. Your doctor replies

    The doctor presses theirs and answers in English or French.

  3. Both languages, side by side

    Every message appears in both languages. Tap play to hear it read aloud, or tap a message to fix a word.

Your health card, in the doctor's language

Write your medicines, allergies and conditions once, in your own words. The doctor sees them translated at the start of every visit, so the most important facts never depend on pronunciation.

  • Turn the phone around for a large-text view the doctor can read.
  • Medicine names on your card help the app hear them correctly.

It tells you when it isn't sure

Doses, numbers, drug names and unclear audio are highlighted so you can check them with your doctor before you leave.

  • Confirm a highlighted part with “It's correct”, or tap to correct it.
  • Only the corrected message is translated again.

Go home knowing what to do next

After the visit, get a plain-language summary in your language: why you went, what the doctor said, your medicines, tests, what to do at home and when to come back.

  • Your next step waits on the home screen.
  • Search, reread and share past visits.

Private by design

Your visits are about your health. We built the app so that they stay with you.

Stored on your phone

Conversations, summaries and your health card live only on your device. There is no account to create.

Recordings deleted

Each voice clip is deleted as soon as its message is ready. Translate-only visits save nothing at all.

Nothing kept on our server

Our server passes audio and text to the translation provider and keeps no copy. Its logs never contain what was said.

You decide

Turn on auto-delete, delete one visit, or delete everything permanently — at any time.
